// HOW TO PLAY

  1. 01

    PICK

    Choose a game from the list — like Sabaho Tahadi or Kelmet El-Serr.

  2. 02

    SPLIT

    Play as teams (2 vs 2) or one-on-one (1 vs 1), and name your teams.

  3. 03

    HOST

    One player is the host: they hold the phone and read out the questions and answers.

  4. 04

    WIN

    Play round by round while the app keeps score. In Sabaho Tahadi, the first to win 3 of 5 rounds takes it.

// THE GAMES

01

Sabaho Tahadi

A five-round challenge. Each round is introduced with its own rules before it starts, and the first side to win three rounds wins.

02

Kelmet El-Serr

A team-versus-team game played round by round, with each team’s score on screen as you go.
