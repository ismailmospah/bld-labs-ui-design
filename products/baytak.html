// PRODUCT 01 — CO-LIVING PLATFORM

BAYTAK

A shared-housing platform for Egypt. Renters find a place and know who they’ll live with before they pay; owners choose who moves in; brokers manage every apartment, bed and commission from one app. Our goal: turn a chaotic market into an organised one.

// THE PROBLEM

  • Listings without real photos or a clear price — every detail is a phone call
  • Deposits paid in cash with nothing in writing
  • Moving in with people you know nothing about
  • Owners and brokers tracking rent and empty beds on paper or in their heads

// HOW IT WORKS

  1. 01

    LIST

    An owner or broker lists the place with real photos, the price and what the rent includes.

  2. 02

    VIEW

    Renters book a free viewing, visit, and meet the people already living there.

  3. 03

    BOOK

    They pick a specific bed and pay online — the deposit is held until they move out.

  4. 04

    MANAGE

    Rent, payments and empty beds are tracked automatically for the owner and broker.

// FOR EVERY SIDE
