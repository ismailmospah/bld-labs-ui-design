// WHO IT’S FOR

  • Arabic speakers who want to recite the Qur’an correctly
  • Non-Arabic speakers learning Tajweed from the start
  • Anyone memorising the Qur’an who needs steady review
  • Learners who want a real teacher alongside the app

// WHAT’S INSIDE

01

Tajweed lessons

Built on the classic text Tuhfat al-Atfal — Noon Sakinah and Tanween, Ghunnah, Meem Sakinah, the rules of Laam and more — one short lesson at a time.

02

Practice that sticks

Audio examples from the Qur’an, true-or-false, choose-the-answer and complete-the-verse exercises, plus a review at the end of every chapter.

03

Hifz path

Memorise surah by surah in themed units. Each unit ends with a test, and a mastery test unlocks when the surah is done.

04

Spaced review

Memorised verses come back for review at the right time, with reminders when a review is due.

05

Learn with a teacher

Browse Tajweed and Hifz teachers, open their profiles and message them on WhatsApp.

06

Motivation

XP, levels, daily streaks and a leaderboard, with gentle reminders and a weekly progress report.

// WHAT WE BUILT

01

Learning design

Turning a traditional Tajweed curriculum into short, bite-sized lessons and exercises.

02

Mobile app

A bilingual Flutter app with Google sign-in, progress synced to the cloud and smart reminders.

03

Admin dashboard

A dashboard to update lessons, manage teachers and send announcements without shipping a new app version.
