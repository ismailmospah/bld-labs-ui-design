What Is the Qalqalah App? Learn Tajweed and Memorize the Quran in One App

Qalqalah is an app for learning the rules of Tajweed and for memorizing and reviewing the Quran, in short daily lessons. Here is everything you need to know before you download it: who it is for, what is inside, and how it works.

Who is Qalqalah for?

  • Beginners who have never studied Tajweed.
  • Non-Arabic speakers: the app is in English and Arabic.
  • Anyone memorizing the Quran who needs a plan and regular review to make it stick.
  • Learners who want a teacher to correct their recitation alongside the app.

The Tajweed path: one short lesson a day

Qalqalah's Tajweed lessons are based on the classic text Tuhfat al-Atfal, divided into short lessons: noon sakinah and tanween, ghunnah, meem sakinah, the rules of lam, and more. Each lesson covers one rule with its examples.

After every rule you practice with recited examples from the Quran: true or false, choose the answer, and complete the verse, with a short explanation after each answer and a review at the end of every chapter.

The memorization path: surah by surah

A map of surahs you move through one after another, in short units that each end with a test, and a mastery test once you finish the surah. You only start a new surah once you've mastered the last one.

Review that comes back right on time

The verses you've memorized come back for review at the right moment, and the app reminds you when a review is due. Every correct review pushes the next one further out, until the memorization is solid. This is called spaced review, one of the most effective ways to make memorization last.

A teacher when you need one

The app guides you step by step, but Tajweed is learned by listening to and reciting for a teacher. That's why Qalqalah lists Tajweed and Hifz teachers: you can open their profiles and message them directly on WhatsApp to correct your recitation.

What keeps you going

  • Points, levels and daily streaks, plus a weekly leaderboard.
  • Memorization groups with friends: you study the same lessons and encourage each other.
  • A badge for every surah you memorize, and a gold badge once you've mastered it through review.
  • Gentle reminders and a weekly progress report.

How much does it cost, and which devices does it run on?

Qalqalah is free to download on iPhone from the App Store and on Android from Google Play, in English and Arabic. Sign in with your account and your progress is saved to the cloud, so you can continue from any device.

Who made Qalqalah?

Qalqalah is built by BLD Labs, a digital product design and development studio. We turned a traditional Tajweed curriculum into short lessons and exercises, and built a dashboard alongside it to update lessons and manage teachers without waiting for a new app release.
