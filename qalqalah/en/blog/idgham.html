Idgham With and Without Ghunnah: The Difference, with Examples

Idgham turns two letters into one. But sometimes it comes with ghunnah and sometimes without, and it has a few specific exceptions. Here is the whole difference, with examples.

3 min read · Qalqalah team

In this article

  1. What is idgham?
  2. The letters of idgham: «يَرْمَلُونَ» (yarmaloon)
  3. Idgham with ghunnah: the letters of «يَنْمُو» (yanmu)
  4. Idgham without ghunnah: lam and ra
  5. When there is no idgham

What is idgham?

Linguistically, idgham means inserting one thing into another.

In Tajweed, it is when a letter with sukun meets a voweled letter and they become one letter with a shaddah. In this chapter: the noon sakinah or tanween merges into the letter after it.

The letters of idgham: «يَرْمَلُونَ» (yarmaloon)

  • ي
  • ر
  • م
  • ل
  • و
  • ن

Ya, Ra, Meem, Lam, Waw and Noon

وَالثَّانِ إِدْغَامٌ بِسِتَّةٍ أَتَتْفِي يَرْمَلُونَ عِنْدَهُمْ قَدْ ثَبَتَتْ

لَكِنَّهَا قِسْمَانِ قِسْمٌ يُدْغَمَافِيهِ بِغُنَّةٍ بِيَنْمُو عُلِمَا

إِلَّا إِذَا كَانَا بِكِلْمَةٍ فَلَاتُدْغِمْ كَدُنْيَا ثُمَّ صِنْوَانٍ تَلَا

وَالثَّانِ إِدْغَامٌ بِغَيْرِ غُنَّهْفِي اللَّامِ وَالرَّا ثُمَّ كَرِّرَنَّهْ

“The second is idgham, with six letters gathered in «yarmaloon». They are of two kinds: one merges with ghunnah, known by «yanmu», unless both are in one word, then do not merge, as in «dunya» and «sinwan». The other is idgham without ghunnah, in lam and ra.”Tuhfat al-Atfal

Idgham with ghunnah: the letters of «يَنْمُو» (yanmu)

If noon sakinah or tanween is followed, in the next word, by one of the letters of «يَنْمُو», the noon merges into it with a ghunnah of about two counts.

Complete and incomplete

Idgham into noon and meem is complete, because the noon disappears entirely, sound and quality. Into ya and waw it is incomplete, because the noon disappears but its quality, the ghunnah, remains.

Idgham without ghunnah: lam and ra

If noon sakinah or tanween is followed by lam or ra, the noon merges into it completely, with no ghunnah, and the lam or ra takes a shaddah.

When there is no idgham

1. When both are in the same word

If the noon sakinah and a letter of «يَنْمُو» are in one word, the noon is pronounced clearly. This is called izhar mutlaq (absolute izhar), and it occurs in four words in the Quran:

  • دُنْيَا
  • صِنْوَانٌ
  • قِنْوَانٌ
  • بُنْيَانٌ

2. Places specific to the Hafs narration

  • «يسٓ ۚ وَٱلْقُرْءَانِ ٱلْحَكِيمِ» and «نٓ ۚ وَٱلْقَلَمِ»: the noon is pronounced clearly in Hafs from Asim, by way of al-Shatibiyyah.
  • «وَقِيلَ مَنْ ۜ رَاقٍ» (Al-Qiyamah · 27): no idgham, because of the brief pause (sakt) on the noon.
Tip

Don't memorize these as abstract rules: listen to them from a skilled reciter and repeat them. Your ear will hold on to them faster.
