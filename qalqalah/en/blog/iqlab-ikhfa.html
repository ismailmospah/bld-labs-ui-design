Iqlab and Ikhfa Haqiqi: Explanation and Examples

Two rules that share the ghunnah and differ in everything else: iqlab has one letter, ikhfa has fifteen. Here is how to tell them apart and how to pronounce them.

Iqlab

Linguistically, iqlab means turning something over. In Tajweed, it means turning the noon sakinah or tanween into a concealed meem, with ghunnah, when it is followed by ba.

  • ب

One letter: ba

وَالثَّالِثُ الْإِقْلَابُ عِنْدَ الْبَاءِمِيمًا بِغُنَّةٍ مَعَ الْإِخْفَاءِ

“The third is iqlab before ba: it becomes a meem, with ghunnah and concealment.”Tuhfat al-Atfal

How to spot it in the Mushaf

A small meem (ۢ) is written above the noon or the tanween to mark iqlab. It happens within one word, across two words, and with tanween:

Tip

Iqlab is three steps in one moment: turn the noon into a meem, conceal the meem by closing the lips lightly, and hold the ghunnah for two counts.

Ikhfa haqiqi

Linguistically, ikhfa means concealing. In Tajweed, it means pronouncing the noon sakinah or tanween in a state between izhar and idgham, without a shaddah, while keeping the ghunnah, when it is followed by one of the ikhfa letters.

It is called haqiqi (“true”) because the concealment is fully realized in it, and to distinguish it from the ikhfa shafawi of meem sakinah.

The fifteen letters of ikhfa

They are the letters left after the letters of izhar, idgham and iqlab. The author of the Tuhfah coded them in the first letter of each word of this line:

وَالرَّابِعُ الْإِخْفَاءُ عِنْدَ الْفَاضِلِمِنَ الْحُرُوفِ وَاجِبٌ لِلْفَاضِلِ

فِي خَمْسَةٍ مِنْ بَعْدِ عَشْرٍ رَمْزُهَافِي كِلْمِ هَذَا الْبَيْتِ قَدْ ضَمَّنْتُهَا

صِفْ ذَا ثَنَا كَمْ جَادَ شَخْصٌ قَدْ سَمَادُمْ طَيِّبًا زِدْ فِي تُقًى ضَعْ ظَالِمَا

“The fourth is ikhfa, required before the remaining letters: fifteen of them, whose codes I have placed in the words of this line.” The third line is the code: the first letter of each of its words is an ikhfa letter.Tuhfat al-Atfal

The ghunnah: how long, heavy or light

The ghunnah in iqlab and ikhfa lasts about two counts. The ghunnah of ikhfa follows the letter after it: it is heavy (tafkhim) before the elevated letters (ص ض ط ظ ق), as in «yansurkum» and «min qablika», and light (tarqiq) before the others, as in «mundhir» and «yunfiqun».

Watch out

The most common mistake in ikhfa: pressing the tip of the tongue where the noon is made, as in izhar. In ikhfa the tongue moves toward the next letter, and the ghunnah comes from the nose.

Iqlab and ikhfa are the third and fourth rules of noon sakinah and tanween.
