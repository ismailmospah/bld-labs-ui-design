Why “Qalqalah”?

Even our name
is a Tajweed lesson.

Qalqalah is the slight echo a letter makes when it carries a sukun, so it is heard clearly. It applies to five letters, gathered in the phrase «قُطْبُ جَدٍّ» (qutbu jadd). Pick a letter to see an example from the Quran.

ٱقْرَأْ

Al-Alaq · 1

The qaf has a sukun in the middle of the word, so it takes the minor qalqalah.

Frequently asked questions

Before you start.

Is the app free?

Yes. Download Qalqalah for free from the App Store or Google Play and start your first lesson right away.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes. Qalqalah is on iPhone through the App Store, and on Android through Google Play.

Is it suitable if I don't speak Arabic?

Yes. The app is available in English and Arabic and starts with you from zero.

Which curriculum are the Tajweed lessons based on?

On Tuhfat al-Atfal by Imam Sulayman al-Jamzuri, divided into short lessons and exercises with examples from the Quran.

Do I need a teacher?

It isn't required: the app guides you step by step. If you'd like someone to correct your recitation, you can contact Tajweed and Hifz teachers on WhatsApp.

Is my progress saved if I change phones?

Yes. Sign in with your account and your progress is saved to the cloud, so you can pick up where you left off.
