// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

Property management shouldn’t be this hard.

Old systems and manual work create inefficiency, mistakes and frustration. Contracts sit in different places, rent is chased by phone, maintenance requests get lost, and owners never get a clear view of their income.

THE SOLUTION

Everything you need to manage property professionally, in one place.

An owner app, a tenant app and an admin dashboard on one platform, each designed around its role: units and contracts, rent tracking and reminders, maintenance from request to repair, shared documents, announcements and direct messages.

// WHAT WE FIXED

  • Scattered documents — contracts, invoices and warranties stored in several places and easy to lose
  • Weak communication — calls and WhatsApp with no record between management, owners and tenants
  • Unclear finances — income and expenses not linked, and reports that arrive late or wrong
  • Maintenance chaos — lost requests, manual follow-up and no clear history of repairs
  • Tenants struggling to follow up on maintenance, reach management and find important documents
  • Disconnected systems — properties, rent and maintenance spread across tools and files

// KEY FEATURES

01

Property management

A central dashboard for every property, unit and tenant, with live status and occupancy insights.

02

Rent tracking

Automatic rent collection, payment reminders, invoicing and full financial reports in one place.

03

Maintenance management

Track work orders, assign tasks to the facilities team, follow progress and keep a detailed service history.

04

Document management

Secure cloud storage for contracts, leases, permits and compliance documents, with version control.

05

Announcements

Send notices to all your tenants at once — a planned water cut, a repair, anything they need to know.

06

Tenant messaging

Talk to tenants directly from the app instead of scattered calls and chats.

// BUILT FOR EVERY ROLE

01

Portfolio owner

Runs many units or a whole compound and handles rent, maintenance, documents and daily contact with owners and tenants — and needs one system with a clear view and full control.

02

Owner

Owns one or more units and wants to follow rent, income, maintenance and documents easily from anywhere, without relying on calls and manual follow-up.

03

Tenant

Lives in a unit and wants an easy way to handle everyday needs: request and follow maintenance, receive announcements, pay rent and reach the lease and management.
