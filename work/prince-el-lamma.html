// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

Group games that work with one phone and no setup.

At a get-together nobody wants to read rules, deal cards or install five apps. The games had to start in seconds, work with one phone passed around, and keep the room laughing.

THE SOLUTION

Pick a game, split into teams, hand the phone to a host.

A playful Arabic interface built for one host holding the phone: choose a game and its rounds, pick 2 vs 2 or 1 vs 1, name the teams, and let the app introduce each round and keep the score.

// WHAT IT HAD TO BE

  • Free, with no ads
  • Easy to use from the first tap
  • Playable with one phone and a host
  • Teams (2 vs 2) or head to head (1 vs 1)
  • Arabic, funny and full of character

// THE GAMES

01

Sabaho Tahadi

A five-round challenge. Each round is introduced with its own rules, and the first side to win three rounds wins.

02

Kelmet El-Serr

A team-versus-team game played round by round, with each team’s score on screen.

03

Who am I?

A round where players guess who they are from the clues the host reads out.

04

Choose the rounds

Pick which rounds to play before the game starts, so every night feels different.
