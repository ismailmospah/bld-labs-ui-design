// CASE STUDY — UX RESEARCH + REDESIGN

ZABEHATY

Zabehaty is an e-commerce app with 500K+ users that sells fresh meat and more, with options for preparation and delivery. One of its key features lets you buy a product and send it as a gift, with a personal message and packaging. The brief was a redesign; we started with UX research to find out why checkout felt tiring and why almost nobody used the gifts feature.

// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

A gifts feature nobody used, and a checkout people found tiring.

The business wanted a redesign. We argued for research first: redesigning without a diagnosis risks optimising the wrong problems. Framed as a way to cut rework and drive revenue rather than a delay, the research phase got the team’s buy-in.

THE SOLUTION

Research first, then a redesign aimed at the real problems.

Usability testing, interviews, an affinity diagram, a journey map and customer-support data pointed to two root causes: most users didn’t know Gift Cards existed, and checkout had too many steps and unclear information. The redesign makes gifting visible and cuts checkout to two steps for returning users.

// WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND

  • Most users didn’t know the Gift Cards feature existed — it was built, just hard to find
  • Gift cards and physical products were mixed together in the cart
  • Users couldn’t tell which saved address an order was going to
  • Card details re-entered on every purchase; Apple Pay and “add a new card” hard to spot
  • Too many steps and too much information to complete a purchase
  • Support tickets about changing the address, signing up and OTP, and picking a delivery time

// WHAT WE CHANGED

01

Gift onboarding

A dedicated onboarding screen introduces Gift Cards to new users.

02

Home banner

A prominent “Send gifts” banner on the home screen, so existing users see the feature without searching.

03

Gifts in their own tab

Gift cards separated from physical products in the cart, so each item is easy to identify.

04

A simpler gift flow

Choose the gift, the card and the message, then the delivery details — or let the recipient add their own address — in fewer steps.

05

Clear delivery address

The selected address sits at the top of the cart, so orders stop going to the wrong place.

06

Better payment methods

Apple Pay first, saved cards, a visible “add a new card”, plus Tabby, Tamara and cash on delivery.

07

One-tap checkout

Saved address and card for returning users: the order takes two steps and one confirmation tap.

// THE RESEARCH

01

Usability testing

Five current users in the UAE, over 30, who had bought before but never used Send Gifts — tested on the gift cards and checkout flows for task success, time, errors, misclicks and satisfaction.

02

Interviews

Users talked about their experience with the app — what they order most, what they like and where they got stuck, from sign-up and OTP to delivery addresses and notifications.

03

Affinity diagram & journey map

Insights grouped for gifts, checkout and the home page, then mapped into a journey with what users did, thought and felt at each stage.

04

Customer support data

Existing tickets reviewed for recurring complaints: changing the delivery address, signing up, choosing an address and time, and completing a purchase.

// WHAT WE DELIVERED

01

UX research

Research plan, usability sessions, interviews, support-data review and synthesis into problems and design goals.

02

Gifts redesign

Onboarding, a home banner, a gifts tab in the cart and a shorter gift flow with cards, messages and delivery options.

03

Checkout redesign

Clear address selection, improved payment methods and a one-tap checkout for returning users.

04

App UI

A refreshed home page and 20+ screens: onboarding, sign-up and OTP, address and map, product and order tracking.
