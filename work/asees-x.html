// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

HR still runs on paper, spreadsheets and chat groups.

Attendance lives in spreadsheets, leave requests get lost in WhatsApp, and employees never know their remaining balance or why their salary changed. HR spends the day answering the same questions.

THE SOLUTION

One clear app for the employee, one live view for HR.

A live work-hours timer with one-tap clock-in and clock-out, a visual leave balance, monthly attendance at a glance and a transparent payslip breakdown. Arabic and RTL-first, backed by a landing page that sells the product to companies.

// WHAT WE FIXED

  • Attendance tracked by hand in spreadsheets
  • Leave requests lost in WhatsApp threads
  • Employees unsure of their leave balance or why their pay changed
  • HR answering the same questions all day

// KEY FEATURES

01

Attendance · الحضور

A live work-hours timer, one-tap clock-in and clock-out, and a monthly summary of present, late and absent days.

02

Leave · الإجازات

Remaining balance at a glance, leave history by type, and every request tracked from submission to approval.

03

Payroll · الرواتب

Net salary with a full breakdown of basic pay, allowances and deductions, plus past payslips to download.

04

Profile & Docs · ملفك

Personal and job details in one place, with employment documents like the contract ready to download.
