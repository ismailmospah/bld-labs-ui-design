// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

Shoppers couldn’t find, compare and buy fast enough.

Users often struggle with finding products easily, comparing options and completing purchases quickly, leading to abandoned carts and frustration.

THE SOLUTION

Organised, searchable, and a checkout without friction.

Otlob offers organised product categories, smart search and filters, clear product details and a smooth checkout process to make shopping faster and easier.

// RESULTS

  • Reduced cart abandonment
  • Faster product discovery and checkout
  • Higher customer satisfaction and repeat purchases
  • A more enjoyable, stress-free online shopping experience
