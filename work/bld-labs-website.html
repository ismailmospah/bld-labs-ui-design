// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

A studio that sells thinking needs a site that shows it.

We don’t start with pixels. We start with research, strategy and deep problem definition — then we design and build products that make sense for the business and the user. A portfolio of pretty screens wouldn’t say that; the site had to.

THE SOLUTION

A terminal-inspired site built around how we work.

Pixel display type, a green-on-black terminal look and one long scroll: the promise, what we believe, who we are, the services, the proof, the process and a call to action — with learning paths and a project brief page alongside.

// WHAT THE SITE HAD TO DO

  • Say what BLD Labs does in one line
  • Lead with how we think: research and strategy before visuals
  • Show proof — work, products shipped and clients
  • Explain the process from discovery to launch
  • Keep “start a project” one step away on every screen
