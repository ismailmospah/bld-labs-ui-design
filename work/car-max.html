// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

Renting a car meant guesswork and too many steps.

Customers face difficulties when renting a car due to limited availability visibility, unclear pricing, complex booking steps and a lack of trust in the rental process.

THE SOLUTION

Clear listings, transparent pricing, an easy booking flow.

CarMax offers a smart and seamless car rental experience with clear car listings, transparent pricing and an easy booking flow. Users can browse available cars, compare options, choose the rental duration and complete their reservation in just a few simple steps.

// RESULTS

  • A faster, smoother booking experience
  • More user trust through transparent pricing
  • Less booking friction and fewer drop-offs
  • Higher customer satisfaction and repeat rentals
