// THE BRIEF

THE CHALLENGE

A motion studio that grew into a full agency.

Top Tech started as a motion graphics studio in Saudi Arabia, moved into content, and today runs strategy, campaigns and performance marketing under one roof. The website had to tell that story and show the agency it is now, not the studio it was.

THE SOLUTION

A bold, motion-led site that ends in a brief.

Dark, high-contrast pages with particle motion and big type: the agency’s evolution in three chapters, six services, client messages shown as chats, proof in numbers and a contact page that asks for everything needed to reply within 24 hours.

// WHAT THE SITE HAD TO DO

  • Tell the story from motion studio to full-service agency
  • Make six services clear at a glance
  • Prove results with clients’ own words and real numbers
  • Turn visitors into a complete brief, answered within 24 hours
  • Work in English and Arabic for clients in Saudi Arabia and Egypt
