// FAQ

I only have an idea. Is that enough to start?

Yes. Most of our MVP projects start with an idea and a conversation. The first phase is discovery: we research the problem and the users with you before anything is designed or built.

How much does an MVP cost and how long does it take?

It depends on the scope of the first version. After a discovery call we send a scoped proposal with a timeline, so you know exactly what you get before we start.

Can the MVP help me raise investment?

We design a clickable prototype and a working first version you can use in investor and user conversations, so you show the product instead of describing it.

What happens after the MVP launches?

We stay on after launch: we measure how the product performs and help you plan and build the next iterations.
