// FAQ

Why research before design?

Because great products are built on understanding, not assumptions. Research tells us what users actually need, so the design solves the right problem the first time.

Do you design for both web and mobile?

Yes. We design web platforms, dashboards and mobile apps, and we can design one product across all of them.

Can you also build what you design?

Yes. The same studio takes the design into development, so nothing gets lost between design and code.

What do I get at the end?

The research findings, user flows, final UI, an interactive prototype and a design system with handoff specs for development.
