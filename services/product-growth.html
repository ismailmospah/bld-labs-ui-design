// FAQ

Can you work on a product another team built?

Yes. We start by reviewing the product — and the codebase where needed — then recommend whether to improve what exists or rebuild parts of it.

Do we have to redesign everything?

No. The audit shows where the real problems are, so we focus on the changes with the biggest impact first.

How do you decide what to improve first?

From research and usability testing with real users, balanced with your business goals.

Can you keep improving the product over time?

Yes. We can work with you continuously: ship, measure and optimize in iterations.
